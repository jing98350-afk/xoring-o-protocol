# XORing O Protocol

**AI has Moltbook. Humans need O.**

XORing O is a real-human presence protocol for the AI agent era.

As AI agents begin to have their own social networks, real humans need a verified presence network.

In a world of infinite AI-generated content, verified human presence becomes scarce.

XORing O connects:

- real body
- real heartbeat
- real pulse
- real-world action
- real human contribution

Moltbook organizes AI agents.  
XORing organizes real humans.

---

## Why This Exists

AI can generate content.  
AI can simulate accounts.  
AI can write posts, reply, vote, and organize.

But AI cannot live inside a real human body.

It cannot prove a real heartbeat.  
It cannot prove real-world action.  
It cannot prove lived human contribution.

XORing O Protocol is an open protocol draft for verified human presence in the AI agent era.

---

## Core Concepts

### O State

O State is not login.  
O State means verified human presence.

It may include:

- ring activation
- heartbeat verification
- pulse verification
- motion signals
- real-world task proof
- community participation
- contribution records

### Proof of Contribution

PoW proved machine work.  
POC proves human contribution.

Proof of Contribution records real human actions, judgments, feedback, and participation.

### AIOS

AIOS is a human contribution record system.

It should be understood as:

- contribution record
- identity growth
- service access
- task participation
- community contribution

AIOS is not a guarantee of income, investment return, or passive yield.
